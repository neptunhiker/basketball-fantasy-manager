{% load i18n %} {% comment %} Loads one of the player list's charts (nba:player-chart) for the filters in #player-list-filters. Context: chart_url, view. It asks only while it is displayed -- a chart folded away on a phone, or the season not on show, costs nothing -- and only for filters it has not drawn yet. It looks again when the chart is opened or the season switched (`chartOpen` and `chartView`, from the page's card), after every filter swap (`htmx:replaced-in-history`, the event the Excel link follows too), and after a resize, since widening past `md` shows the chart unasked. The request goes out through `htmx.ajax` rather than a custom trigger, so it does not depend on htmx having processed the page yet. A failed one forgets what it asked for, so the next look tries again. While a new chart is on its way the old one stays, dimmed, rather than blinking out. {% endcomment %}
